# Template: a listening check on an Analog Four MKII. The listen text has three parts: what to hear,
# `allowed:` what the person may do on the unit by hand (or nothing), and `needs:` the audible
# precondition, which the section proves on screen first. The text is the instruction: a different
# instruction (said in the thread, say) is a different plan, with its own emulator pass.
format = 1
name = listening check
unit = a4
refs = refs/a4-listen
out = runs
note = ~T SETUP active, unsaved: T1 trig on step 1; stopped

[start]
wait 3
tap NO
wait 0.5
tap NO
wait 0.5
tap NO
wait 1.0
# OSC1 steps between OSC1 (P 1/2) and NOISE (P 2/2), and a track key keeps the page, so OSC1 is
# entered from another page (FLTR): its first press then always opens P 1/2.
tap T1
wait 0.5
tap FLTR
wait 0.6
tap OSC1
wait 0.6
# the start check: T1's OSC1 page 1/2, by its knob labels and dials (rows 8-63). The title
# bar names the page only for a moment before it goes back to the kit's name.
expect-screen t1-osc1-page 2 rows=8-63

[setup: T1 on step 1]
say proves setup: one T1 trig on step 1 (grid recording: a key on the live screen adds no trig)
tap RECORD
wait 0.6
tap 1
wait 0.3
tap RECORD
wait 0.6

[L1 T1 plays steadily]
say proves evidence: the ear of the person, with the pattern proven playing first
tap PLAY
wait 1
# the play indicator only (a triangle playing, a square stopped)
expect-screen playing 2 rows=2-6 cols=119-125
listen T1 plays once per bar, steady, with no clicks or dropouts. allowed: nothing on the unit; the volume on your own speakers only. needs: the pattern playing (the play indicator was checked on screen). Say finished when done.
tap STOP
wait 1
