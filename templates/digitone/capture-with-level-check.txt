# Template: an audio capture on a Digitone behind a level check, so a silent take stops the run
# instead of passing. The Digitone screen shows nothing of PLAY or RECORD (no page tried on OS 1.44
# differs between playing and stopped), so the level check is the only proof that the pattern plays.
# In digiemu track 1 sounds (a trig on step 1 at 120 BPM peaked at about -23 dBFS on Main and Track 1),
# so the emulator pass says something about sound, but its level is not the unit's.
#
# Overbridge channels, counted from 1: Main L/R 1,2; Track 1 L/R 3,4; Tracks 2-4 5-10; Input 11,12.
# The level check compares a short take with this plan's own reference take (level-ref.wav in its
# refs folder), recorded once on the unit from the same steps and kept. audio-compare fails on
# silence; audio-dropouts alone does not (silence has no dropouts).
format = 1
name = capture with level check
unit = digitone
refs = refs/digitone-capture-with-level-check
out = runs
audio-tools = C:/path/to/elkbench/audio
note = ~T SETUP active, loaded new (A01 UNTITLED, 120.0); stopped

[start]
# Home first, then track 1 TRIG page from there. The page title row (rows 0-9) shows "Trig
# Parameters" only for a moment, so the check compares rows 10-63: they differ from the SYN1 page
# (cols 18-127) and from T2 TRIG page (the track digit, rows 24-31 cols 5-9), so one check proves
# both the page and the track.
tap NO
wait 0.4
tap NO
wait 0.4
tap NO
wait 0.4
tap NO
wait 1.0
tap T1
wait 0.4
tap TRIG
wait 1.5
expect-screen t1-trig-page 2 rows=10-63

[setup: one T1 trig on step 1, the pattern playing]
say proves setup: the sequencer starts track 1 (a pattern trig, not a MIDI note)
tap RECORD
wait 0.5
tap 1
wait 0.5
tap RECORD
wait 0.5
tap PLAY
wait 1.5

[level check (hardware)]
say proves works: track 1 sounds at its reference level on its own channels (3,4) before any take
audio-record 3 level
audio-compare level-ref level 3,4

[take (hardware)]
say proves works: the take, compared with its reference
audio-record 10 take
audio-dropouts take 3,4
audio-compare take-ref take 3,4
tap STOP
wait 1
