# Template: a listening check on a Digitone. The listen text has three parts: what to hear,
# `allowed:` what the person may do on the unit by hand (or nothing), and `needs:` the audible
# precondition. The text is the instruction: a different instruction (said in the thread, say) is a
# different plan, with its own emulator pass.
#
# The Digitone screen shows nothing of PLAY (no page tried on OS 1.44 differs between playing and
# stopped), so the section proves on screen what the screen can show (track 1 TRIG page, entered
# from home), and a level check before it proves on the unit that track 1 sounds.
format = 1
name = listening check
unit = digitone
refs = refs/digitone-listen
out = runs
note = ~T SETUP active, loaded new (A01 UNTITLED, 120.0); stopped

[start]
# Home first, then track 1 TRIG page from there. The page title row (rows 0-9) shows "Trig
# Parameters" only for a moment, so the check compares rows 10-63: they differ from the SYN1 page
# (cols 18-127) and from T2 TRIG page (the track digit, rows 24-31 cols 5-9), so one check proves
# both the page and the track.
tap NO
wait 0.4
tap NO
wait 0.4
tap NO
wait 0.4
tap NO
wait 1.0
tap T1
wait 0.4
tap TRIG
wait 1.5
expect-screen t1-trig-page 2 rows=10-63

[setup: T1 on step 1, playing]
say proves setup: one track 1 trig on step 1, the pattern started
tap RECORD
wait 0.5
tap 1
wait 0.5
tap RECORD
wait 0.5
tap PLAY
wait 1.5

[level check (hardware)]
say proves works: track 1 sounds (channels 3,4) before the person is asked to listen
audio-record 3 level
audio-compare level-ref level 3,4

[L1 track 1 plays steadily]
say proves evidence: the ear of the person, with the page proven on screen and the sound by the level check
expect-screen t1-trig-page-playing 2 rows=10-63
listen Track 1 plays once per bar, steady, with no clicks or dropouts. allowed: nothing on the unit; the volume on your own speakers only. needs: the pattern playing (the level check passed; the screen shows no play state) on the track 1 TRIG page (checked on screen). Say finished when done.
tap STOP
wait 1
